## 📌 Submission Guidelines for Students
- Capture screenshots in high-resolution PNG format (1920x1080 resolution recommended).
- Ensure browser dev tools or unnecessary background windows are omitted.
- Paste captured images into the designated placeholder blocks prior to final printing if real UI prints are requested by faculty reviewers.
